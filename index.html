The Kingdom of Eggerton

A tiny kingdom,
and a very large problem.

Eggerton is busy. Eggerton is always busy. The court is full, the ledgers are thick, and everyone can tell you how hard they are working.

What nobody can tell you is whether any of it is making money.

So the books are called before the court — and a small kingdom learns the difference between busy and profitable, the true cost of loyalty, and what exactly to do when the chickens get fancy.
